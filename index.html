        Before you start

        Some practices here use fast breathing and long breath holds. They can make you lightheaded, and in rare cases you can black out without warning.

        • Only seated or lying down. Never in or near water, in the bath, or while driving.
        • Skip fast breathing and breath holds altogether if you are pregnant, or have epilepsy, heart disease, high blood pressure or a fever. Choose Resonance, Cyclic Sighing or Alternate Nostril, or a No holds option instead.
        • Stop whenever you like. If you feel dizzy or unwell, or the urge to breathe gets strong, breathe normally.
        • There is no target to beat. Breathe now ends any hold.
