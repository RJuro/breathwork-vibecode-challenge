Pranayama · 15 minutes

Turn the tide

For the scratchy-throat day. Humming, nasal breathing and three rounds of long, quiet holds, in the spirit of Dylan Werner's pranayama.

    Headphones in. Sit or lie down. Never in water or while driving.

    Complete ·

    Rest now.

      • Warm drink, early night. Sleep is the best-supported thing you can do for a cold.
      • A few slow hums through the day are a nice reminder. Space them out; they work best that way.
      • If a fever arrives, skip the rounds. Rest, or use the Gentle setting.

      Before you start

      This flow includes fast breathing followed by breath holds. That can make you lightheaded, and in rare cases you can black out without warning.

      • Only seated or lying down. Never in or near water, in the bath, or while driving.
      • Skip the rounds (choose Gentle in settings) if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, heart disease or high blood pressure, or have a fever.
      • Tingling hands or face is common and passes. If you feel unwell, breathe normally and stop.
      • Never force a hold. Tap Breathe now whenever you need to.

      Settings

      IntensityGentle drops the fast breathing. Use it if you're feverish or new to holds.
